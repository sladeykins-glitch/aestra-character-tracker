For Iris
Break the sealDrag the letter opener across the wax

If you are reading this, then the wind has already begun to change.

Do not be frightened by that. You have always been braver than you believe, though I remember how often you wished you were not.

There are things I cannot tell you plainly. Not because I do not trust you, but because I know what happens when certain words are spoken too soon.

Remember the old gardens beneath the palace. Remember the places where we used to hide when we wished to escape our duties. There is more truth beneath those stones than there ever was within the throne room.

You will meet people you do not yet know.

Some will wear familiar faces.
Some will speak with familiar voices.
And some will feel strangely like home.

Trust the faces you see when you dream.

When the time comes, do not follow the crown.
Do not follow the voice.
Follow the ones who remember.

I wish I could tell you everything.

Perhaps, in another turning of the wheel, I already have.

With all the love I have left,
Iris
